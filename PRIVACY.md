# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 16, 2026

## Data Collection

LinkedIn DM Scheduler does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers.

## Local Storage

All data — including scheduled messages, campaigns, message templates, settings, and recipient profile URLs — is stored locally in your browser using `chrome.storage.local`. This data never leaves your device.

## LinkedIn Access

The extension accesses LinkedIn pages only to compose and send messages that you have explicitly scheduled or initiated. It does not read your LinkedIn messages, connections, feed, or any other data beyond what is necessary to send the messages you create.

## Data Sharing

No data is sold, shared, or transferred to third parties.
